Why Allora

The open toolchain, assembled into one instrument.

Open FPGA development is powerful, but the pieces are scattered. Allora connects the editor, simulator, constraints, synthesis, place-and-route, programming, logs, and project state without hiding the tools underneath.

Virtual FPGA V0.1

Your RTL becomes something you can touch.

Allora discovers your top-level ports with Yosys and runs the same Verilog or SystemVerilog design through a persistent Verilator model. No scripted imitation and no hardware required.
